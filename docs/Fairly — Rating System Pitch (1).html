The brief
What the system has to do
01
Rating and flags
A 1 to 3 star scale, with set flag reasons.
02
Anti-abuse
Stop spam and targeted down-voting, including by groups.
03
Privacy
Students see only their own rating. Staff see everything.
The system
Three parts
Voting Gate
What students see and do: sign in, rate someone, see their own rating.
Data Engine
Weighs each vote, detects abuse patterns and calculates scores.
Staff Dashboard
Where staff review held votes, look at students and change settings.
A vote goes from the Gate to the Engine to the Dashboard. Staff decisions go back to the Engine.
01 · Voting Gate
How a student uses it
  1. Sign in with your 42 Amman alias and a code sent to your email.
  2. Home shows your rating, your labels, your monthly trend and Community votes left.
  3. Search for a student and open their page. No score is shown there.
  4. Rate: pick a category, pick 1 to 3 stars, then labels or a flag.
  5. Confirm. You can undo the vote for 5 minutes.

01 · Voting Gate
Limits and privacy
Limits
  • 3 Community votes a week
  • No limit on After evaluation votes
  • The same student once a week, per category
  • The week resets on Sunday
What a student sees
  • Their own rating, after 5 different raters
  • The labels they received, and their trend
  • Never who rated them, comments or flags about them, or anyone else’s rating
02 · Data Engine
When a vote counts, and how much
When it counts
  • 3 and 2 star votes count right away
  • Every 1-star vote waits for staff
  • Votes the engine marks high risk also wait
How much it counts
  • Voters with better integrity count more
  • Rating the same person again counts less
  • Voters who give everyone the same stars count less
  • Older votes count less
02 · Data Engine
Detecting abuse
Pattern
What it looks for
Pile of low votes
Several low votes on one student in a short time
Voting wave
A group voting the same way on the same students
Pair voting
Two students rating each other up in sync
Too frequent
Votes unusually close together or in unusual volume
Extreme voter
Someone who only gives 1 or 3 stars
Low risk counts. Medium counts and is shown to staff. High is held. Each alert carries a plain reason, for example: “4 low votes in 2 hours from students who usually vote together.”
02 · Data Engine
Calculating a student’s score
  • A weighted average of the counted votes.
  • With few votes, the score stays close to the campus average.
  • Shown to the student only after 5 different raters.
  • The rating and label counts update once a week, so no change points to one vote.
  • A monthly trend, for months with at least 3 ratings.
  • Six dimension scores, for staff only.

03 · Staff Dashboard
The review queue
Order
What it holds
1. Urgent
Aggressive behavior, Bad Evaluator, Didn’t understand their own code
2. High risk
Votes held because of a detected pattern
3. 1-star votes
Every other 1-star vote
4. Repeat alerts
The same flag on one student 3 times in a month
5. Notices
Votes that counted but look unusual
Within each level, the oldest case comes first. Votes caught by the same pattern show up as one case.
03 · Staff Dashboard
Reviewing a case
Each case shows
  • The votes and their explanations
  • Why the engine flagged it
  • The voter’s integrity and history
  • The student’s summary and related votes
Staff can
  • Confirm, reject or dismiss
  • Decide a whole group at once
  • Open an in-person conversation
  • Add private notes
The voter’s name stays hidden until a staff member chooses to see it, and that is logged.

03 · Staff Dashboard
Rules for staff
  • Penalties can require an in-person conversation.
  • For urgent flags, staff talk to both students. The flagger stays anonymous.
  • At least two staff members agree on any penalty.
  • Rejected votes are kept, marked as rejected.
  • Decisions, settings changes and identity views are all logged.
