# Peer Rating System: Key Ideas

## 1. Rating Collection

- **Confirmed interactions only.** Ratings and flags require a confirmed interaction between the two students, and ratings are only possible within a time window. Both parties can rate each other.
- **Ratings and flags are separate.** A rating only affects the score. A flag goes directly to staff, is not limited by the rating window, and never changes a score automatically.
- **Flags are for extreme cases.** Staff question both the flagger and the flagged student, which discourages misuse.
- **Criteria-based form.** Ratings use separate criteria, and a low rating requires a reason chosen from a preset list, with an optional note visible only to staff.

## 2. Scoring Engine

- **Cumulative scoring.** The history of ratings decides the score. A low score appears only after a series of low ratings, never from one or two.
- **Time decay.** Older ratings lose weight, so the score reflects current behavior and students can improve.
- **Repeated-rater weighting.** Similar ratings from the same person count as roughly one rating, which limits retaliation and mutual boosting.
- **Overall score plus per-criterion scores.** Students can see what lowered their score, and they see it both when it is low (to improve) and when it is high (as motivation).
- **Controlled visibility.** Scores are shown only after a minimum number of ratings and are revealed in a weekly window. An early notice is allowed only when the cumulative score declines, never because of a single rating.

## 3. Staff Controls

- **Staff decide the meaning of scores.** The engine produces signals only and has no automatic consequences.
- **No penalty without a conversation.** A penalty is applied only after a face-to-face discussion in which the student can present their case and appeal.
- **Two-person decisions.** Penalties are agreed by at least two staff members, since no higher appeal body exists.
- **Written record.** Each decision is recorded with its reason and the student's statement.
- **Individual review.** Because flags are rare, each one can be reviewed properly instead of being handled in bulk.
